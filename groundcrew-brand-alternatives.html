00 — The sitting brand, briefly

Good system, wrong signal.

  1. The mark says "stop." Wands crossed overhead is the marshalling signal for STOP / emergency stop — not all-clear. For a tool whose whole job is dispatch, the logo is telling the fleet to halt.
  2. At 16–36px it collapses into a generic ×. In the exact surfaces it will live in — Slack threads, CI checks, terminal tabs — × reads as failed. The avatar next to "PR ready for review" looks like a red flag.
  3. The craft is solid but the costume is rented. Orange-on-ink monospace is the default 2020s dev-tool uniform. Nothing here is wrong; nothing is ownable either.
OPTION A Follow Me

The follow-me truck: the checkered vehicle that meets every arriving aircraft and leads it to its stand. groundcrew does literally this — meets every task and leads the agent to its worktree. The checker doubles as a finish flag, so the same mark means "dispatched" on the way out and "done" on the way back. High-vis industrial print: paper, safety orange, hazard stripe.

OPTION D move ahead

Same wands, corrected grammar. Crossed overhead means stop — so lead with the beckon instead: the "move ahead" signal a marshaller uses to wave an aircraft onto its stand. A head-dot makes it a person rather than a glyph, and the wands beckon on a 1.6s loop. Orange, ink, and mono all carry over from the sitting brand untouched. This is the minimal diff — and the crossed pose isn't wasted, it's demoted to the HOLD state where it's finally telling the truth.

Recommendation

Keep the name — it's the best part of the current brand. D — Move Ahead is the minimal-diff fix: same palette and type, wands rotated to the signal that actually means dispatch, and the old crossed mark demoted to the HOLD state where it's semantically correct. Of the clean-sheet options, C — Taxiway is the most ownable: no other dev tool looks like airport ground signage, it's legible at 16px by color alone, and the sign grammar extends to every task state the tool will ever need to render in Slack. A is the safest crowd-pleaser; B is the most fun in --watch mode but the scope mark is illegible as a favicon.
