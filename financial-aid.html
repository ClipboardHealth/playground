Pathways

Choose your path

Use this as a starting map. Confirm admission requirements, prerequisites, clinical schedules, accreditation, board approval, total cost, and aid deadlines directly with each school.

CNA / Basic Nursing Assistant

Entry pathway

Good for workers entering healthcare, meeting nursing-program prerequisites, or building patient-care hours.

  • Ask the local American Job Center whether the program is WIOA-approved.
  • Check City Colleges, Instituto, College of DuPage, Harper, Oakton, Moraine Valley, and other community colleges.
  • Budget for background check, physical, immunizations/titers, books, scrubs, transportation, and exam fees.

LPN / Practical Nursing

License pathway

Good for CNAs and other healthcare workers who want a faster licensed-nursing step before RN.

  • Check practical-nursing programs at Malcolm X College, Harper College, and other Illinois-approved providers.
  • Ask whether WIOA, MAP, school aid, or the Illinois Nursing Education Scholarship can apply.
  • Confirm clinical schedule and whether you can continue working while enrolled.

RN / ADN

RN pathway

Good for LPNs and CNAs moving toward RN licensure through an associate degree in nursing.

  • Check City Colleges of Chicago / Malcolm X, Instituto College, College of DuPage, Moraine Valley, Oakton, Harper, and other local programs.
  • Confirm prerequisite courses, TEAS or entrance testing, CNA requirements, waitlists, and NCLEX pass-rate disclosures.
  • Ask early about WIOA eligibility and scholarship deadlines; many deadlines close months before classes begin.

RN-to-BSN

Advancement pathway

Good for current RNs seeking BSN completion for advancement, hospital roles, public health, leadership, or graduate study.

  • Compare online and hybrid options such as UIC and other Illinois universities.
  • Ask about transfer credits, part-time pacing, clinical/project requirements, and scholarships for working nurses.
  • Consider loan-repayment or service programs if you work in qualifying shortage or public-service settings.

Questions

Frequently asked

Plain-language answers for workers comparing options.

Should I apply for aid before or after I choose a school?

Do both early. Complete FAFSA or the Illinois Alternative Application as soon as possible, then ask each school what aid, scholarships, and payment options apply to your program. For WIOA, confirm the program is approved before enrolling.

What costs should I budget beyond tuition?

Common costs include prerequisites, entrance exams, background check, drug screen, physical, immunizations/titers, BLS/CPR, books, supplies, scrubs, shoes, transportation, childcare, exam fees, NCLEX prep, and state licensure fees.

Can I keep working while in nursing school?

Many students work while enrolled, but clinical schedules can be rigid and may change each term. Ask the school for sample schedules and build a shift plan before committing.
